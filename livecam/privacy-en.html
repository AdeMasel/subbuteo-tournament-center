Privacy policy — Table Soccer Live Cam

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (version 1.0.7) · Author: Antonio de Masellis

Table Soccer Live Cam is a video directing app for table football. It runs on your phone and has no servers of its own.

What the app uses

  • Camera and microphone: to film, record and stream the match, only after you press «START».
  • Network: for live streams to the platforms you choose (YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, Instagram, TikTok, RTMP/SRT servers) and for the optional connection to Table Soccer Tournament Center on your network, even on a Wi‑Fi without Internet (replays go straight to the directing computer, without any outside service).
  • Storage: videos, thumbnails, teams, logos and the broadcaster logo you choose for the on-air graphics are saved on your phone, in the folder you choose.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect personal data and uses no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It sends nothing to the author. The error log stays on the phone and you share it yourself, if you want, with «Send the error log»; it never contains stream keys or passwords.

Live streams and third-party services When you go live, video and audio go to the platform you configured, under its own terms and privacy policy. Stream keys are encrypted on the phone with the Android Keystore. The optional YouTube or Twitch sign-in only reads the stream key; access tokens are not stored.

Updates (website version only) Once a day the app reads a small public file with the latest version number; it sends no data.

Deletion You can delete videos from the Library; uninstalling the app removes all its internal data.

Contact: the author, through the Table Soccer Tournament Center website.
